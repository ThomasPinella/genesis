# genesis

A simulated universe built from simple primitives, to see what emerges.

- [Experiment 1: Freeze-out](experiments/01-freeze-out/): particles that interact by attention,
  starting hot with random genomes and cooling on one energy budget. Do elements and bonds freeze out?
- [Experiment 2: Energy](experiments/02-energy/): the same world with a sweeping sun, stored energy,
  eating along attention and self-propulsion. Do absorbers, eaters, foraging and driven patterns appear?
- [Experiment 3: Geometry](experiments/03-geometry/): particles get a facing and six sticky faces, so
  bonds have direction. Do chains, rings and sheets form, and does any shape assemble copies of itself?
- [Experiment 4: Face chemistry](experiments/04-face-chemistry/): each face of a particle gets its own
  chemistry, so a chain's spine and its sides can bond differently. Can a chain act as a mold for a copy of itself?
- [Experiment 5: Shaking light](experiments/05-shaking-light/): part of the light a bonded particle absorbs
  pushes its bonds apart, so the world warms where the sun shines. Can a template's partners sort out and a copy let go?
- [Experiment 6: Coarse-grained attention](experiments/06-coarse-grained-attention/): attention reaches far and sees
  distant regions as one blend of their members. Do groups act as units, and does a group's arrangement show from afar?

## Series B

A second series that keeps only polarity and energy flow: no attention, no genome.

- [B1: The polar soup](series-b/b1-soup/): balls with four + or − spots, cooling slowly. Do they build
  small shapes that repeat, one big net, or a crystal?
- [B2: Day and night](series-b/b2-cycles/): the same soup in a bath that is warm by day and cool by
  night. Does the cycle keep many kinds of small shape alive, and do any survive the days?
- [B3: Teamwork bonds](series-b/b3-teamwork/): a ball holds each partner harder the more partners it
  has. Do well-connected groups outlast the days without the soup knitting into one net?
- [B4: Caps](series-b/b4-caps/): some balls carry a single spot, like hydrogen. Can groups finish,
  using up every spot, and do complete molecules outlast the days?
- [B5: Two levels](series-b/b5-two-levels/): caps hold twice as hard as other bonds. Do capped
  molecules last while weak bonds between them build a second, looser level?
- [B6 (probe): Copying](series-b/b6-copying/): blocks with a strong backbone and a letter that pairs
  only with letters. Does a chain gather a copy at night and release it by day?
