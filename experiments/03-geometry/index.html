Geometry

The world of experiment 2, now with direction. Every particle has an orientation and sticky patches on some of its six faces; which faces are sticky is set by the law for each element. Two particles bond only where patches face each other, and they turn to make that happen. Do shapes form, and does any shape assemble copies of itself?

    Universe

    The law seed picks the physics, including the light's color. The world seed picks the starting conditions. These restart the universe.
