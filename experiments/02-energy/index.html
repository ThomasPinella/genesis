Genesis · Experiment 2

Energy

The world of experiment 1, now with a sun. Light comes down from the ceiling in a band that sweeps across the world, so every place gets day and night. Particles absorb it by how well they match its color, eat from whatever they attend to, and spend what they store pushing themselves around. Do absorbers, eaters, foraging and driven patterns appear? Color shows the element; brighter means more stored energy.

Universe

The law seed picks the physics, including the light's color. The world seed picks the starting conditions. These restart the universe.
